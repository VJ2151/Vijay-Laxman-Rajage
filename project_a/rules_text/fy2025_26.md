# Statutory Income Tax Rules — FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)
Source: Income Tax Department of India, Finance Act 2025 proposals / amendments to Section 115BAC.
Scope: Resident Individual, Salaried Income only.

---

## 1. New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC as amended by Finance Act 2025)

- **[S115BAC-STD-DED] Standard Deduction**: Rs 75,000 maintained for salaried individuals under Section 16(ia).
- **[S115BAC-SLAB-2025] Revised Tax Slabs & Rates**:
  - Up to Rs 4,00,000: Nil
  - Rs 4,00,001 to Rs 8,00,000: 5%
  - Rs 8,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 10%
  - Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 16,00,000: 15%
  - Rs 16,00,001 to Rs 20,00,000: 20%
  - Rs 20,00,001 to Rs 24,00,000: 25%
  - Above Rs 24,00,000: 30%
- **[S87A-NEW-2025] Section 87A Rebate & Marginal Relief**:
  - Full rebate up to Rs 60,000 if taxable income does not exceed Rs 12,00,000 (tax becomes Nil).
  - Marginal Relief: If taxable income exceeds Rs 12,00,000, tax payable shall not exceed the amount by which taxable income exceeds Rs 12,00,000. Relief ceases when normal slab tax <= (taxable_income - 12,00,000).
- **[S115BAC-DED-EXCL] Deductions Not Allowed**: Chapter VI-A deductions, HRA, and Section 24(b) interest remain excluded under the new regime.

---

## 2. Old Tax Regime (Unchanged)

- **[OLD-STD-DED] Standard Deduction**: Rs 50,000.
- **[OLD-SLAB-NORMAL] Tax Slabs (<60 years)**: 0-2.5L Nil, 2.5-5L 5%, 5-10L 20%, >10L 30%.
- **[OLD-SLAB-SENIOR] Tax Slabs (60-79 years)**: 0-3L Nil, 3-5L 5%, 5-10L 20%, >10L 30%.
- **[OLD-SLAB-SUPER] Tax Slabs (80+ years)**: 0-5L Nil, 5-10L 20%, >10L 30%.
- **[S87A-OLD] Section 87A Rebate**: Full rebate up to Rs 12,500 if taxable income <= Rs 5,00,000 (no marginal relief).
- **[SEC-10-13A] HRA Exemption**: Minimum of actual, rent-10% basic, or 50%/40% basic.
- **[SEC-24B] Self-Occupied Interest**: Max Rs 2,00,000.
- **[SEC-80C] 80C Limit**: Max Rs 1,50,000.
- **[SEC-80CCD1B] NPS Limit**: Max Rs 50,000.
- **[SEC-80D] Health Insurance**: Rs 25k/50k self, Rs 25k/50k parents.

---

## 3. Surcharge, Marginal Relief, Cess & Rounding

- **[SURCHARGE-RATES]**: Surcharge thresholds at 50L (10%), 1Cr (15%), 2Cr (25%). New regime capped at 25%; Old regime has 37% above 5Cr.
- **[SURCHARGE-RELIEF]**: Surcharge marginal relief at all thresholds.
- **[HEALTH-EDU-CESS]**: 4% on tax plus surcharge.
- **[SEC-288A / SEC-288B]**: Rounding of taxable income and tax payable to nearest multiple of Rs 10.
