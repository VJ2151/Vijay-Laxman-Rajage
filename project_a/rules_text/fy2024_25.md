# Statutory Income Tax Rules — FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)
Source: Income Tax Department of India (incometax.gov.in), Finance Act 2024, Finance (No. 2) Act 2024.
Scope: Resident Individual, Salaried Income only.

---

## 1. New Tax Regime (Default per Section 115BAC(1A))

- **[S115BAC-STD-DED] Standard Deduction**: Rs 75,000 allowed for salaried individuals under Section 16(ia) as amended by Finance (No. 2) Act 2024.
  - Source: https://incometaxindia.gov.in / Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 Sec 16(ia).
- **[S115BAC-SLAB] Tax Slabs & Rates**:
  - Up to Rs 3,00,000: Nil
  - Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 7,00,000: 5%
  - Rs 7,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 10%
  - Rs 10,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 15%
  - Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 15,00,000: 20%
  - Above Rs 15,00,000: 30%
  - Source: Section 115BAC(1A), amended by Finance (No. 2) Act 2024.
- **[S87A-NEW] Section 87A Rebate & Marginal Relief**:
  - Full rebate up to Rs 25,000 if taxable income does not exceed Rs 7,00,000 (tax becomes Nil).
  - Marginal Relief (Proviso to Sec 87A for 115BAC): If taxable income exceeds Rs 7,00,000, tax payable shall not exceed the amount by which taxable income exceeds Rs 7,00,000. Relief ceases when normal slab tax <= (taxable_income - 7,00,000), which occurs at Rs 7,27,778.
  - Source: Proviso to Section 87A, Finance Act 2023 / Finance (No. 2) Act 2024.
- **[S115BAC-DED-EXCL] Deductions Not Allowed**: Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D), Section 10(13A) HRA, and Section 24(b) interest on self-occupied house property are NOT allowed under new regime.
  - Source: Section 115BAC(2).

## 2. Old Tax Regime (Optional per Section 115BAC(6))

- **[OLD-STD-DED] Standard Deduction**: Rs 50,000 under Section 16(ia).
  - Source: Section 16(ia), Income Tax Act 1961.
- **[OLD-SLAB-NORMAL] Tax Slabs (Age < 60)**:
  - Up to Rs 2,50,000: Nil
  - Rs 2,50,001 to Rs 5,00,000: 5%
  - Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
  - Above Rs 10,00,000: 30%
  - Source: First Schedule, Finance Act.
- **[OLD-SLAB-SENIOR] Tax Slabs (Age 60 to 79 — Senior Citizen)**:
  - Up to Rs 3,00,000: Nil
  - Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 5,00,000: 5%
  - Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
  - Above Rs 10,00,000: 30%
- **[OLD-SLAB-SUPER] Tax Slabs (Age 80+ — Super Senior Citizen)**:
  - Up to Rs 5,00,000: Nil
  - Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
  - Above Rs 10,00,000: 30%
- **[S87A-OLD] Section 87A Rebate**:
  - Full rebate up to Rs 12,500 if taxable income does not exceed Rs 5,00,000.
  - Cliff effect: No marginal relief exists under old regime for income exceeding Rs 5,00,000.
  - Source: Section 87A, Income Tax Act 1961.
- **[SEC-10-13A] HRA Exemption (Rule 2A)**:
  - Minimum of:
    1. Actual HRA received
    2. Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary
    3. 50% of basic salary (if in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% (other cities)
  - Source: Section 10(13A) read with Rule 2A of Income Tax Rules 1962.
- **[SEC-24B] Self-Occupied House Property Interest**:
  - Maximum deduction of Rs 2,00,000 for interest on borrowed capital for self-occupied house.
  - Set off against salary income.
  - Source: Section 24(b) second proviso.
- **[SEC-80C] Chapter VI-A — Section 80C**:
  - Maximum deduction: Rs 1,50,000.
  - Source: Section 80CCE.
- **[SEC-80CCD1B] NPS Additional Contribution**:
  - Maximum deduction: Rs 50,000 (over and above 80C limit).
  - Source: Section 80CCD(1B).
- **[SEC-80D] Health Insurance Premium**:
  - Self, spouse, children: Rs 25,000 (Rs 50,000 if taxpayer age >= 60).
  - Parents: Rs 25,000 (Rs 50,000 if parents_senior = True).
  - Source: Section 80D(2).

## 3. Surcharge, Marginal Relief, Cess & Rounding (Common Provisions)

- **[SURCHARGE-RATES] Surcharge on Income**:
  - Old Regime:
    - Rs 50L to 1 Crore: 10%
    - Rs 1 Crore to 2 Crore: 15%
    - Rs 2 Crore to 5 Crore: 25%
    - Above Rs 5 Crore: 37%
  - New Regime:
    - Rs 50L to 1 Crore: 10%
    - Rs 1 Crore to 2 Crore: 15%
    - Above Rs 2 Crore: Capped at 25% (37% rate abolished in new regime).
  - Source: Finance Act / Section 115BAC.
- **[SURCHARGE-RELIEF] Surcharge Marginal Relief**:
  - Total tax + surcharge on income above threshold (50L, 1Cr, 2Cr, 5Cr) shall not exceed:
    (Total tax + surcharge payable at the threshold) + (Income exceeding the threshold).
  - Applied before Health and Education Cess.
- **[HEALTH-EDU-CESS] Health and Education Cess**:
  - 4% on (Tax after 87A rebate + Surcharge after marginal relief).
  - Source: Finance Act.
- **[SEC-288A] Rounding of Total Income**:
  - Total taxable income rounded to nearest multiple of Rs 10.
  - Source: Section 288A of Income Tax Act 1961.
- **[SEC-288B] Rounding of Tax Payable**:
  - Net tax payable / refundable rounded to nearest multiple of Rs 10.
  - Source: Section 288B of Income Tax Act 1961.
